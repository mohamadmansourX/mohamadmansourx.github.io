StellarVisionX

Team Vision Beyond, NASA Space Apps Challenge, October 2023.

StellarVisionX is a telescope stationed 42 million km from the Sun. From there it has a better chance of discovering comets, and it can collect and analyse solar flare data.

Cameras

Two cameras face sideways instead of forward, toward the plane where planets and comets orbit. That helps spot comets as they head toward the Sun.

Components

Derived from the Parker Solar Probe: a sun shield, solar panels, a solar array cooling system, a double wide field imager and a communication system.

Motivation

In early August 2023 a Japanese amateur astronomer found comet Nishimura by accident, when it was 1.0 AU from the Sun. It came as close as 43 million km. We decided to build a telescope that orbits the Sun to improve the chances of discovering comets.
